# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE VIDEO SPEED

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses the `activeTab` and `scripting` permissions to set the playback speed of video and audio on the current tab, only when you click the icon, and the `storage` permission to remember your last chosen speed. it has no access to your browsing history and makes no network requests. nothing is ever sent anywhere.
