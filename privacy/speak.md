# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE READ ALOUD

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses the `activeTab` and `scripting` permissions to read the selected or page text on the current tab when you click, and your browser's built-in speech engine to read it aloud locally. the `storage` permission remembers your voice, speed, and pitch. no text or audio is ever sent to any server.
