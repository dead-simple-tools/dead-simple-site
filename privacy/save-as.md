# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE SAVE AS

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. images and videos you save are downloaded directly to your device. a local history of recent saves (filename, format, byte size, timestamp) is kept in your browser only and never leaves it.

it uses the `contextMenus`, `downloads`, `activeTab`, `storage`, and `scripting` chrome permissions to add right-click menu entries, re-encode images via canvas in the page context, and trigger downloads.
