# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE POMODORO

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses the `alarms` permission to run the countdown when the popup is closed, the `notifications` permission to alert you when a phase ends, and the `storage` permission to remember your timer settings and progress. it makes no network requests and nothing is ever sent anywhere.
