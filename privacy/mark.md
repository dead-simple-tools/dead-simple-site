# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE HIGHLIGHTER

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to anyone. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses host access to show and save your highlights on the pages you use it on, and the `storage` permission to keep those highlights on your own device, tied to the page address. it has no account and makes no network requests. your highlights never leave your machine.
