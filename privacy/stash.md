# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE TAB STASH

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to anyone. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses the `tabs` permission to read the tabs you choose to stash and to reopen them when you restore, and the `storage` permission to keep your stashed lists on your own device. it does not sync to any server and makes no network requests. your stashed tabs never leave your machine, and you can export them to a file you control at any time.
