# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE CLEAN LINK

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses the `activeTab` permission to read the current tab's url when you click the icon, remove known tracking parameters locally, and optionally reload that tab on the cleaned url. it has no background process and makes no network requests. nothing is ever sent anywhere.
