# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE PICTURE IN PICTURE

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses the `activeTab` and `scripting` permissions to put the current tab's video into a picture-in-picture window, only when you click the toolbar icon or press the shortcut, and the `storage` permission to remember your options locally. it has no access to your browsing history and makes no network requests. nothing is ever sent anywhere.
