# privacy policy — DEAD SIMPLE QR CODE

this extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. it operates entirely on your device.

it uses the `activeTab` permission to read the current tab's url only when you tap "use current tab url", and the `storage` permission to remember your error-correction preference. qr codes are generated locally with a bundled encoder. nothing is ever sent anywhere, and the codes contain only the text you provide, with no redirect or tracking.
